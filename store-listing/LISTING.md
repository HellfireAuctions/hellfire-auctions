# Hellfire Auctions - Shopify App Store listing (copy/paste ready)

## App name (max 30 characters)
Hellfire Auctions

## App introduction (max 100 characters)
Run live timed auctions in your store with real-time bidding, countdowns and automatic invoicing.

## App details (max 500 characters)
Turn any product into a live auction. Shoppers place maximum bids and the app bids for them automatically, so prices climb fairly and the highest bidder always wins. Live countdowns show on every product card, outbid and "1 hour left" emails pull bidders back, and when time runs out the winner is invoiced through Shopify automatically. Prices and end times lock once listed, so every bidder is treated the same. Works with any theme.

## Feature list (max 80 characters each)
- Automatic proxy bidding: bidders set a maximum, the app bids for them
- Live bid and countdown on every product card, down to the second
- Outbid and 1-hour-left emails bring bidders straight back
- Winners invoiced automatically through Shopify when the auction ends
- Works with any Shopify theme, no code or theme edits needed

## Testing instructions for Shopify reviewers (paste into the "Testing instructions" field)
1. Install Hellfire Auctions. The app opens to the Auctions page.
2. Online Store > Themes > Customize > App embeds: turn on "Hellfire Auctions Runtime" and save. (It works with any theme; no theme code changes.)
3. In the app, create an auction: enter a title, starting bid, start date/time and duration, add an image, and click Create Auction. The app creates the product, tags it, and adds it to a "Live Auctions" collection automatically.
4. On the storefront, open the Live Auctions collection: each auction card shows the live current bid, bid count and a countdown to the second. The theme's normal price and Add to Cart are hidden for auction products only.
5. Open the auction product. Sign in as a customer (bidding requires a customer account) and place a maximum bid. Sign in as a second customer and bid higher: the price rises automatically (proxy bidding) and, on the Blaze or Inferno plan, the first customer receives an "outbid" email.
6. When the auction ends, the winner is automatically sent a Shopify draft-order invoice.
7. Plans: the app's "Plans & upgrades" page offers Spark (free, 10 auctions/month), Blaze ($10/month, 90 auctions, emails) and Inferno ($25/month, unlimited, HOT badge). On a development store, upgrades are test charges.
Tip: to see an auction end quickly, choose a start time a few minutes ahead and the shortest duration, or use an existing auction in our test data.
